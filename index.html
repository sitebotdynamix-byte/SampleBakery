Meet Dana Whitcombe

Dana grew up in a Wyoming kitchen where the oven was always on in winter. After ten years baking in Denver and Portland, she came home in 2014 and opened Sample Bakery with a secondhand mixer and one starter named Gus.

Today a team of six bakes for ranch families, students, hikers and wedding parties. We buy flour from Great Plains mills and eggs from a farm outside town.

  • 12 years baking in Laramie
  • Our promise If it’s not right, we’ll remake it or refund it, no questions.
  • Local We donate unsold bread to the community pantry every Sunday.

How ordering works

  1. Look at the case

    Walk in or check our daily list by phone. Loaves are baked in the same order each morning.

  2. Hold it or order ahead

    Call to hold bread until 2 p.m. Cakes need five days’ notice.

  3. Pick up warm

    Come to the counter and say your name. We’ll box it and add a bag for the road.

  4. Tell us how it went

    Anything off? Tell us at the counter and we’ll make it right.

Common questions

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

We offer a vegan oat cookie daily and a flourless chocolate cake on request. Our kitchen handles wheat, so we can’t promise a gluten-free environment.

How far ahead should I order a cake?

Five days for custom cakes, two days for six-inch layer cakes. Call and we’ll check the calendar with you.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. We take cards and cash, and we can send a payment link for cake orders.

Do you deliver?

We deliver wedding and event cakes within 30 miles of Laramie. Bread and pastries are pickup only.
